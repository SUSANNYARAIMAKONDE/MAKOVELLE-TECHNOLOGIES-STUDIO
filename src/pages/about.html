About Makovelle

We make technology practical.

Makovelle Technologies Studio is a software development and technology solutions company based in {{site.country}}. We help organisations solve operational challenges through carefully designed websites, custom software, digital systems and business automation.

Who we are

Built for organisations that run on real work.

Many schools and businesses still manage critical work on paper, in disconnected spreadsheets, or with software that was never designed for the way they operate. The result is familiar: hours lost to manual reconciliation, records that are hard to find, and decisions made without a clear picture.

We exist to change that. Makovelle is a studio that listens first. We learn how your organisation actually runs, identify where time and money are being lost, and design technology that fits — then we stay with you to keep it working.

Our focus is on private schools and educational institutions, small and medium-sized businesses, and any organisation ready to digitise its operations and work more efficiently. Whether you need a first professional website or a system at the centre of your daily operations, we approach the work with the same care.

Our mission

To make technology practical and accessible to businesses and institutions through reliable digital solutions.

Our vision

To become a trusted technology partner for organisations seeking digital transformation.

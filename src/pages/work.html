Demonstration project

School Fees Management System

A demonstration platform showing student records, fee allocations, payment tracking, outstanding balances and receipt generation.

{{> mock-fees}}
The problem

Many schools track fees in receipt books and spreadsheets. Balances are hard to confirm, reconciliation takes days, and parents wait for answers about what they owe.

Proposed solution

A single, secure system for the bursar's office: fees are allocated by class and term, every payment updates balances instantly, and a receipt is generated the moment money is recorded.

Key features
  • Student records by class
  • Fee structures and allocations
  • Payment recording
  • Outstanding balance tracking
  • Instant printable receipts
  • Collection summaries
Technologies

Interactive demo: HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Proposed production build: React, Node.js and PostgreSQL, with secure user accounts and automated backups.

Private School Website

A demonstration website for Maconte Academy, a fictional private school, showing how we help educational institutions build a professional online presence.

{{> mock-school-site}}
The problem

Parents research schools online before they visit. Schools without a clear, current website lose prospective families to institutions that present themselves better, regardless of the quality of their teaching.

Proposed solution

A polished, mobile-first website that communicates the school's ethos, answers parents' key questions, guides families through admissions and keeps the community informed with news and events.

Key features
  • Admissions information and enquiries
  • Academics and co-curricular pages
  • News, events and calendar
  • Staff-friendly content updates
  • Search-engine optimisation
  • Fast on mobile data
Technologies

HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript, with an optional content management system so school staff can publish updates without technical help.

Concept

Parent & Student Portal

A concept showing how schools can improve communication and give parents secure access to relevant student and account information.

{{> mock-portal}}
The problem

Important information reaches parents late or not at all: notices get lost, balance queries tie up the office and reports are only seen at the end of term.

Proposed solution

A secure online portal, on phone or computer, where each parent sees only their own children's information: fee statements and receipts, attendance, reports and school notices.

Key features
  • Secure parent and student logins
  • Fee statements and receipts
  • Attendance overview
  • Reports and results
  • School notices and events
  • Works on any device
Technologies

Proposed: React, Node.js and PostgreSQL with role-based access control, designed to connect to the school fees and management system.

Welcome

A school built on character and curiosity.

Maconte Academy is an independent day school for learners from Form 1 to Upper 6. Small classes, dedicated teachers and a strong pastoral system mean that no child goes unnoticed.

We believe education is about more than examination results. Alongside rigorous academics, our learners take part in sport, the arts, service and leadership, discovering what they are good at and what they care about.

“Our promise to every family is simple: we will know your child, challenge them, and help them become their best self.”Head of School
  • IntegrityWe do the right thing, even when no one is watching.
  • CuriosityWe ask questions, explore ideas and love learning.
  • ServiceWe use our gifts to make our community better.

Academics

A broad, demanding curriculum.

Learners follow a well-rounded programme in the junior forms, then specialise for O and A Level with guidance from subject teachers and careers advisors.

  • Sciences

    Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Combined Science, with practical laboratory work every week.

  • Mathematics

    Core and Additional Mathematics, plus enrichment and olympiad preparation.

  • Commercials

    Accounting, Business Studies and Economics with real-world case studies.

  • Humanities

    History, Geography and Heritage Studies that build critical thinking.

  • Languages

    English Language and Literature, Shona, Ndebele and French.

  • Technology & Arts

    Computer Science, Art and Design, and Music.

Student life

Every learner finds their place.

Afternoons at Maconte are full: on the sports field, in the music room, at club meetings and in service projects across the community.

  • ◆

    Sport

    Athletics, football, netball, rugby, basketball, swimming and tennis.

  • ◆

    Arts & music

    Choir, marimba ensemble, drama productions and art exhibitions.

  • ◆

    Clubs & societies

    Debate, robotics, chess, Interact and the environmental club.

  • ◆

    Leadership

    Prefects, house captains and a student council with a real voice.

Admissions

Joining Maconte in four steps.

We welcome applications for Form 1 and Lower 6, and for other forms when places are available.

  1. 1

    Enquire & visit

    Send an enquiry or join an open morning to see the school.

  2. 2

    Apply

    Submit the application form with the latest school report and birth certificate.

  3. 3

    Assessment

    Applicants sit short English and Mathematics assessments and meet a senior teacher.

  4. 4

    Offer & enrolment

    Successful families receive an offer letter and enrolment pack.

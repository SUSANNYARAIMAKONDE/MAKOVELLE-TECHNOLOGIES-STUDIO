Demonstration project. Built by Makovelle to show our approach. Maconte Academy and all student names, amounts and records are fictional. The interactive demo runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

Overview

From receipt books to a clear, live picture.

The problem

Fee records spread across receipt books, spreadsheets and bank statements are slow to reconcile and easy to get wrong. Bursars spend days confirming balances, leadership lacks a reliable view of collections, and parents wait for answers.

Proposed solution

One system where fees are allocated by class and term, every payment immediately updates the student's balance, and an official receipt is produced at the moment of payment. Reports on collections and arrears are available at any time.

Who it's for

Private and independent schools, colleges and training institutions that want accurate, efficient fee administration.

Technologies

Interactive demo: HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Proposed production build: React, Node.js and PostgreSQL, with role-based user accounts, audit trails and automated backups.

Key features

Everything the bursar's office needs in one place.

Each feature replaces a manual task with a faster, more accurate step.

  • 01

    Student records

    Every learner's class, guardian and account history, searchable in seconds.

  • 02

    Fee allocation

    Fee structures set per class and term, applied consistently to every student.

  • 03

    Payment tracking

    Cash, bank transfer and mobile money payments recorded against the right account.

  • 04

    Outstanding balances

    See who is paid up, part-paid or overdue, filtered by class or status.

  • 05

    Instant receipts

    A numbered, printable receipt is generated the moment a payment is recorded.

  • 06

    Collection reporting

    Billed, collected and outstanding totals at a glance, for leadership and the board.

Concept project. Maconte Academy is a fictional school created for this demonstration. It is not a client and not a real institution. The live demo runs entirely in your browser; its enquiry form does not send anything.

Overview

A school's first impression, made with care.

The problem

Families compare schools online long before they visit. An outdated site, or none at all, leaves parents with unanswered questions and gives the impression of a school less capable than it really is.

Proposed solution

A clear, elegant website structured around what parents need to know: the school's ethos, academics, facilities, fees information and how to apply. News and events keep the wider community engaged.

Who it's for

Private schools, colleges and educational institutions establishing or renewing their online presence.

Technologies

HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript for speed and reliability, with an optional content management system so staff can update news, events and notices themselves.

Key features

Built for parents, easy for staff.

A school website should answer questions, build confidence and make the next step obvious.

  • 01

    Admissions journey

    Clear requirements, key dates and an enquiry form that reaches the right person.

  • 02

    Academics & activities

    Curriculum, co-curricular programmes and facilities presented with clarity.

  • 03

    News & events

    A calendar and news section that keeps parents and alumni informed.

  • 04

    Mobile-first & fast

    Designed for phones first and optimised to load quickly on mobile data.

  • 05

    Search visibility

    Structured for search engines so families can find the school easily.

  • 06

    Simple updates

    Optional content management so staff can publish without technical help.

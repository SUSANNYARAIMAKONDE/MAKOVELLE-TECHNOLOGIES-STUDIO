Concept project. This portal is a design concept by Makovelle. All names, balances and notices shown are fictional. The live demo needs no real login, runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

Overview

Fewer phone calls, better-informed parents.

The problem

Notices sent home get lost, balance enquiries tie up the office, and parents often see progress only at the end of term. Communication is slow for families and time-consuming for staff.

Proposed solution

A secure portal on phone or computer where each parent sees their own children's fee statements and receipts, attendance, reports and school notices, kept up to date automatically from the school's systems.

Who it's for

Schools that want to strengthen relationships with parents and reduce administrative workload.

Technologies

Proposed: React, Node.js and PostgreSQL with role-based access control and encrypted connections, designed to integrate with a school fees and management system.

Key features

The right information, for the right person.

Privacy and clarity come first: every account sees only what it should.
